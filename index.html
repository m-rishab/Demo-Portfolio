About

I make sure the numbers are right before anyone uses them.

Most of my work has been in the everyday part of analytics: ERP systems, reports that used to break quietly, and dashboards that had to survive an audit. I learned that writing the query is the easy part. The hard part is giving people a number they can trust and use.

At Emerson I rebuilt the reporting on Oracle EBS and Noetix. I cleaned up 10+ Power BI dashboards and 50+ DAX measures so the same metric meant the same thing everywhere, saving six hours of manual reporting every week. I also handled row-level security, SOX ITGC controls, and ServiceNow incidents when something broke.

Now, with HighSpring, I check answers from Google's AI Mode. I score 5,000+ of them against gold ratings, catch the ones that sound confident but are wrong, and track the patterns in Looker Studio dashboards the team uses every day. I also audit Wikipedia and Ballotpedia across three locales with Gemini and Search Grounding, which cut the weekly audit from eight hours to two.

I like work where you can check whether I got it right.
